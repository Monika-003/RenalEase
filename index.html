About RenalEase

Project overview, purpose and limitations.

🫘

RenalEase

Smart Dialysis Monitoring and Patient Care Platform

RenalEase is an engineering project combining a dialysis-related prototype concept with a web interface for displaying example sensor information, organizing session records and demonstrating patient-doctor workflows.

The planned platform includes patient profiles, monitoring visualizations, device registration, session history, alerts and appointment management. Hardware communication and secure backend services are separate development stages.

Medical Safety Disclaimer: RenalEase is an educational engineering prototype and software demonstration. It is not a clinically validated or approved dialysis machine, medical device, diagnostic tool or emergency service. Do not use it to make medical decisions or control treatment.
